Then & Now

Are storms getting worse, or are we counting more? Both can be true, and the records alone can't always tell them apart. This page compares two eras only where NOAA counted the same way in both, and says plainly where it can't. How it works →

Pick two spans of years. For every kind of event NOAA recorded in every year of both, you'll see the yearly average then and now. Counts are recorded events, not storms: more reports can mean more storms, or more radar and trained spotters noticing them.4 Damage is adjusted for inflation to 2024 dollars unless you choose otherwise.
